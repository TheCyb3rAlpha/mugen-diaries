The MUGEN Diaries

Reader of the moon. Finder of the unseen flaw. It sees what the compiler tried to bury, and shows you where the walls run thin.

In plainer words: MUGEN is a static analyzer for compiled Windows binaries, and this is the honest record of building it, the wins, the reverts, and the misses, in the order they happened. The invocation is for effect. The diary is not.
